Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy covers the sirjoshshorts publishing tool ("the tool"), a private application that uploads videos made by sirjoshshorts to the sirjoshshorts accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Who uses the tool

Only the owner of the sirjoshshorts accounts. The tool is not available to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • Access tokens issued by each platform when the owner signs in and grants permission (OAuth).
  • Basic profile information about the owner's own accounts (account ID, name, avatar), only as needed to publish.
  • The videos, titles and captions the owner chooses to publish.

The tool does not collect, read or store data about viewers, followers or any other person.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload and publish the owner's own videos and to check that a post succeeded. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer, inside the automation software that runs the tool. They are not sent to any other service.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's security settings (Google Account permissions, Facebook Business Integrations, TikTok Manage app permissions). On revocation the stored tokens are deleted.

Third-party platforms

Content published through the tool is subject to the policies of YouTube (including the Google Privacy Policy), Meta and TikTok. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service.
